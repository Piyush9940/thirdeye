SCIENTIFIC LIMITATIONS: SENTINEL-5P CH4 INVESTIGATION
===========================================================================

1. The methane comparison uses only 48 matched pixel pairs.
2. The matched observations come from two acquisition dates in September 2019.
3. The matched pixels are not a representative India-wide sample.
4. Spatial matching uses nearby pixels; matched pixels are not necessarily identical ground footprints.
5. Satellite viewing and solar geometry variables are strongly interdependent.
6. The negative solar-azimuth association is predictive and observational, not proof of a physical causal effect.
7. Leave-one-quadrant-out validation uses only four spatial blocks.
8. Two spatial quadrants contain only six observations each.
9. Quadrant boundaries were calculated from the complete matched sample.
10. The pooled spatial-validation R2 uses the overall held-out target mean as reference.
11. The rank-based model results and ppb-based methane results measure different prediction tasks.
12. Two acquisition dates cannot establish a long-term methane trend.
13. The analysis does not independently validate the results against ground-based methane measurements.
