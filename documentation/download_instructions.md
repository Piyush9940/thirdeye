# Satellite & Climate Data Download Instructions

## 1. Copernicus Data Space (Sentinel-5P TROPOMI)
- Portal: [https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/](https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/)
- Product: `L2__CH4___` or preprocessed `L3__CH4___` NetCDF files.
- Bounding Box: West 69.0, South 7.0, East 97.0, North 37.0.

## 2. NASA POWER Meteorological Reanalysis
- Portal: [https://power.larc.nasa.gov/](https://power.larc.nasa.gov/)
- API: Monthly Surface Meteorology.
- Parameters: `T2M`, `PRECTOTCORR`, `RH2M`, `WS10M`.
