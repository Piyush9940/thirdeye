NASA POWER Surface Meteorology 2° Gridded Reanalysis

Contextual Climate Variable Analysis

Investigating the statistical relationships between atmospheric methane column concentration and key surface meteorological forcings from NASA POWER.

Correlation Is Not Causation Statistical associations between climate variables and satellite $X\text{CH}_4$ column concentration do NOT prove that a specific weather factor caused methane emissions. Column concentrations represent composite vertical abundance shaped by broad atmospheric transport, regional winds, and boundary layer thermodynamics.
