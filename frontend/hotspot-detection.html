Scientific Terminology & Source Attribution Caveat In atmospheric observation, an area of high column concentration is classified as a Candidate Area for Investigation or Relative Concentration Cluster, NEVER an "illegal emission point" or "proven emission source". Atmospheric transport, topographic pooling, planetary boundary layer height variations, and broad upwind advection can concentrate methane in regions far removed from actual surface release sites.
