Coordinate Geometry

Grid nodes are positioned on a regular 2° by 2° spherical coordinate lattice across the bounding box [69°E–97°E, 7°N–37°N]. Each node represents a 2° spatial bin center.

Monsoon Data Gap

During the Indian summer monsoon (e.g. August 2021), thick convective cloud cover leads to severe QA masking. Only 11 nodes pass quality filtering, demonstrating genuine satellite data sparsity.

Concentration Integrity

Observed concentrations vary from ~1788 to ~2022 ppb. Unobserved nodes are rendered with dashed borders to prevent misleading interpretations of zero emissions or data fabrication.
