Scientific Methodology & Technical Framework

Comprehensive scientific explanation of data acquisition, quality assessment, spatial binning, meteorological integration, model architectures, and chronological validation protocols.

01 Satellite Data Acquisition Sentinel-5P TROPOMI

Total column atmospheric methane ($X\text{CH}_4$) soundings were acquired from the European Space Agency (ESA) Copernicus Data Space. TROPOMI measures solar backscattered radiance in the shortwave infrared (SWIR Band 7, 2305–2385 nm) with high sensitivity to the lower troposphere where greenhouse gas sources originate.

02 Quality Assessment & Stringent Filtering QA ≥ 0.50

Each TROPOMI pixel contains a rigorous quality value (qa_value) from 0 (bad) to 1 (perfect). Pixels with $\text{QA} < 0.50$ (cloud contamination, snow cover, high solar zenith angles, aerosol scattering artifacts) are filtered out, leaving 73,045 high-fidelity soundings.

03 Spatial Discretization (2° Regular Lattice) 255 Grid Nodes

India's bounding box [69°E–97°E, 7°N–37°N] is discretized into a 2° × 2° spherical grid resulting in 255 discrete spatial nodes. Each node represents the geometric center of a spatial bin and is partitioned into six geographic zones (North-West, North-East, Central-West, Central-East, South-West, South-East).

04 Methane Spatial Aggregation Robust Statistics

Soundings falling within each 2° bin during an observation period are aggregated into median, mean, and standard deviation. Median concentration ($X\text{CH}_4$ in ppb) was selected as the primary target variable due to its resilience against extreme boundary-layer outliers.

05 NASA POWER Meteorological Integration 4 Surface Parameters

Surface meteorology from NASA POWER (Prediction of Worldwide Energy Resources) reanalysis was spatially matched to each grid node: Air Temperature at 2m (T2M), Corrected Precipitation (PRECTOTCORR), Relative Humidity (RH2M), and Wind Speed at 10m (WS10M).

06 Spatial Graph Construction Haversine Distance Weighting

A spatial adjacency matrix was constructed connecting neighboring grid nodes within spherical distance thresholds. Both distance-weighted Gaussian kernel edges and unweighted binary edges were computed to evaluate graph neural network representations.

07 Feature Engineering & Standardization 8 Input Dimensions

The final feature space comprises 8 ordered features: [T2M, PRECTOTCORR, RH2M, WS10M, Latitude, Longitude, Month Sine, Month Cosine]. Features 0–5 are standardized to zero mean and unit variance using strictly training-set statistics. Target values are normalized and later de-normalized to ppb.

08 Machine Learning Experimentation MLP vs GCN Benchmark

Four core models and diagnostic ablations were trained: Original MLP (32 units, ReLU, Dropout 0.2), Distance-Weighted GCN, Unweighted GCN, Month-only diagnostic, and Climate-only ablation. All trained for 250 epochs using Adam optimizer.

09 Chronological Expanding-Window Validation 3 Folds × 5 Random Seeds

To avoid temporal data leakage, models were evaluated strictly forward in time across 3 folds: Fold 1 evaluated on 2021-02, Fold 2 evaluated on 2021-08, and Fold 3 evaluated on 2023-11. Each fold was trained and tested across 5 random seeds (7, 21, 42, 77, 123) yielding 15 verified checkpoints.

10 Evaluation Metrics & Scientific Transparency Negative R² & Underprediction

Performance was measured using MAE, RMSE, $R^2$, and Bias. The metrics demonstrate weak predictive generalization across distant time gaps (negative $R^2$) and negative bias due to Rising national methane baselines. GCN models did not outperform the simple MLP.

11 Dashboard & API Delivery Verified Data Integration

The entire analytical output is published via production-quality REST APIs, interactive Leaflet maps, 10 Plotly charts, AI-assisted grounded retrieval, and downloadable research dossiers.
