Marine & Coastal Boundary Atmospheric Context

Evaluating the impact of peninsular India's maritime interfaces, marine boundary layer humidity, and coastal wind transport on satellite methane soundings.

Coastal Advection

Strong south-westerly monsoon winds transport clean marine air over the Western Ghats, rapidly dispersing continental column plumes and diluting observed concentrations.

Sun-Glint Geometry

Over open ocean, water absorbs shortwave infrared (SWIR). Satellite retrievals require specular sun-glint reflection geometry to achieve sufficient signal-to-noise ratios.

Marine Boundary Layer

High relative humidity (> 75%) along coastal nodes correlates with shallow boundary layers, modifying the vertical pressure-weighting function of column averaging.
