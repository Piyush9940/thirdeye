ABOUT THE PROJECT

Explore Earth. Understand Methane. Discover Environmental Patterns.

Our Mission & Core Vision

Earth Systems Trend Detective was conceived for the NASA Space Apps Challenge to address a fundamental gap in environmental communication: satellite Earth observations and climate datasets are frequently trapped inside complex, massive scientific data formats (such as NetCDF4 and HDF5) that remain inaccessible to ordinary citizens, educators, and community leaders.

Our platform bridges this divide by turning quality-filtered Sentinel-5P TROPOMI satellite measurements and NASA POWER surface meteorology into an open, cinematic, and interactive web experience. We aim to help anyone explore atmospheric methane variations across India with scientific accuracy, clarity, and zero sensationalism.

Scientific Integrity

Our Four Scientific Commitments

Science without honesty is not science. Every visualization and model result on this platform adheres to four strict standards:

1. Concentration ≠ Emissions Physics Grounded

Total column atmospheric methane ($X\text{CH}_4$ in ppb) measures vertical column abundance, not surface emission flux. We never claim satellite observations pinpoint exact industrial leak points or identify illegal polluters without Lagrangian dispersion modeling.

2. Missing Data ≠ Zero Honest Gaps

Grid nodes obscured by monsoon clouds or discarded due to QA filtering (< 0.50) are displayed explicitly as unobserved or missing. We never replace missing data with zeros or invent continuous trends where data is absent.

3. Honest Machine Learning Negative R² Documented

We report out-of-fold performance transparently: negative $R^2$ scores, underprediction bias, and the empirical reality that Graph Neural Networks (GCN) did not outperform simple MLPs. The reference model is labeled "Experimental Reference Model — Not Production Approved".

4. 100% Open Science Full Transparency

All spatial node tables, satellite observations, prediction logs, fold summaries, and evaluation reports are published in open CSV and JSON formats for independent peer review and verification.
