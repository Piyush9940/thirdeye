Secondary Technical Module

ADVANCED RESEARCH & ML LAB

Empirical evaluation of spatial graph neural networks (GCN) and multi-layer perceptrons (MLP) on sparse atmospheric satellite observations.

MANDATORY SCIENTIFIC STATUS: EXPERIMENTAL REFERENCE MODEL — NOT PRODUCTION APPROVED

• Negative $R^2$ scores across evaluation folds confirm that models perform worse than predicting the target mean.
• Graph Neural Networks (GCN) did not outperform the multi-layer perceptron (MLP).
• The month-only calendar diagnostic achieved the lowest MAE (49.04 ppb), showing that simple seasonal baselines dominate sparse spatial signals.
• This model is an exploratory reference artifact for research benchmarking, NOT an operational forecasting engine.
