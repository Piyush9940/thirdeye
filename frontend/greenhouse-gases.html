Multi-Greenhouse Gas Framework Extensibility Roadmap

While this verified release focuses strictly on atmospheric methane ($\text{CH}_4$) from Sentinel-5P, the data pipeline is architected to ingest future validated greenhouse gases and aerosol optical products without altering downstream analytics:

Carbon Dioxide (CO₂) Planned

Targeted sensor: NASA OCO-2 & OCO-3 Lite files. Catalogued during exploratory phase; pending regional spatial co-registration.

Nitrous Oxide (N₂O) Planned

Agricultural and biogeochemical forcer. Dependent on high-resolution reanalysis from Copernicus CAMS models.

Aerosol Optical Depth (AOD) Evaluating

MODIS MCD19A2 AOD data for solar scattering corrections and atmospheric aerosol context.
