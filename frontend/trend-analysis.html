Temporal Pilot Scope 4 Snapshot Months

Temporal Trend Analysis & Sparsity Diagnostics

Investigating atmospheric methane shifts between 2019 and 2023 over India, evaluating data gaps created by monsoon cloud dynamics, and assessing time-series statistical constraints.

Time-Series Integrity: Snapshot Periods vs Continuous Monitoring The pilot dataset contains exactly four discrete observation months (May 2019, Feb 2021, Aug 2021, Nov 2023). It is NOT a continuous monthly time series. Non-parametric trend tests (such as Mann-Kendall or Sen's Slope) require continuous, uniformly sampled records. We display verified snapshot shifts without fabricating intermediate monthly values.
