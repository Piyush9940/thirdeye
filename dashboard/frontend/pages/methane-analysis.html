Essential Scientific Principles & Integrity

1. Concentration ≠ Surface Emissions: Sentinel-5P TROPOMI measures column-averaged dry-air mole fraction (XCH4 in ppb) integrated throughout the atmospheric column. High atmospheric methane concentrations do not directly locate localized point-source emissions without atmospheric transport inversion models.
2. Monsoon Cloud Cover Limitation: Optical soundings require clear-sky conditions (QA ≥ 0.50). In August 2021 (Fold 2), intense monsoon convective cloud cover left only 11 valid soundings across all of India.
3. Southeast India Pilot Gap: Southeast India has 0 valid soundings in this pilot release due to cloud filtering and orbit geometry. Missing data is shown transparently as unavailable, never as zero.
